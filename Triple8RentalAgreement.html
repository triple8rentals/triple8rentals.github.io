1. Rental Property

The Lessor agrees to rent to the Lessee the Appliance(s) in good working order — tested prior to delivery, free from known defects, and including all standard parts for normal operation. The Lessee acknowledges receipt of the Appliance in good working order and accepts it as suitable and functional at the time of delivery.

2. Rental Period

The rental period commences on the start date and continues for the initial term selected. After the initial term, this Agreement will automatically continue on a month-to-month basis unless terminated per Section 9.

3. Rental Fee and Payment

First payment is due on the day of delivery. Late Fee: $10 for any payment more than 5 days past due. Payments more than 30 days past due accrue interest at 8% per annum.

Automatic Recurring: Lessee authorizes Lessor to charge the card on file monthly. If declined, Lessee must update payment info within 5 days to avoid late fees.

Monthly Invoice: Lessee is responsible for timely payment regardless of whether the invoice is received or viewed.

4. Delivery and Installation

Lessor will deliver and install the Appliance. Lessee is responsible for proper electrical, water, gas, and plumbing connections. Upon termination, Lessor will arrange pick-up.

5. Use and Maintenance

Lessee will use the Appliance only for its intended household purpose per manufacturer instructions. No commercial or business use. Lessee is responsible for routine maintenance and any damage from improper use or neglect, including water damage from a leaking hose or drain.

6. Utility Costs

Lessee is solely responsible for all utility costs (electricity, water, gas). If utilities are discontinued, Lessee's obligations remain.

7. Relocation of Appliance

The Appliance must remain at the specified address. Lessee shall not relocate it without Lessor's prior written consent.

8. Access and Inspection

Lessor may access the premises to inspect, repair, or retrieve the Appliance upon 48 hours' notice. This right shall not be unreasonably denied.

9. Termination and Default

A. Initial Term: Lessee shall not terminate early. Early termination incurs liquidated damages of $200.

B. Post-Initial Term: Either party may terminate with 30 days' written notice. Appliance must be returned in good working condition (normal wear and tear excepted).

C. Default: Lessee defaults by missing two consecutive payments (5+ days past due) or otherwise breaching the Agreement. Lessor may retrieve the Appliance after 48 hours' notice. Default fee: $200.

D. Failure to Return: Failure to make Appliance available within 7 days of termination results in a replacement fee of $200/unit ($400 for washer/dryer set).

E. Late and Unpaid Amounts: All amounts due immediately upon notice. Unpaid amounts accrue 8% annual interest and may be referred for collection, with Lessee bearing collection costs.

10. Security Interest

Lessor retains ownership of the Appliance at all times. This is not a purchase, lease-to-own, or installment sale.

11. Limitation of Liability and Indemnification

Lessee assumes all risk of loss, damage, theft, or destruction from delivery until return. Lessor is not liable for injury or damage from use or malfunction, except for Lessor's gross negligence or willful misconduct. Lessee agrees to indemnify and hold harmless Lessor from claims arising out of Lessee's possession or use of the Appliance.

12. Severability

If any provision is found invalid, the remaining provisions continue in full force.

13. Arbitration of Disputes

Any dispute shall be resolved by binding arbitration under AAA rules in Denver, Colorado. The decision is final and binding. Each party bears its own costs, except the prevailing party may be awarded reasonable costs and attorneys' fees.

14. Notices

Notices are properly given in person, by text to the number listed, or by email. Deemed received on the date sent.

Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties. Any amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties.
