Frequently Asked Questions

Do you require a credit check?
No. We do not perform credit checks. Our goal is to make premium appliances accessible to everyone in the Denver area regardless of credit history. A valid debit or credit card is required to be saved on file for automatic monthly payments.
Is there a down payment?
There is no deposit. For a standard set installation in our regular service area with normal access, just your first month’s $69 rent is due at installation. Special access or out-of-area charges may apply; we will tell you beforehand. A valid debit or credit card must be saved for recurring payments.
Will my monthly rental price increase after signup?
No automatic rate increases during your uninterrupted rental. If you sign up for a standard washer and dryer set at $69 per month, that agreed monthly rate remains $69 as long as you continuously rent the set. This is not an introductory promotion.
How soon can I get my appliances?
We pride ourselves on speed. Most customers receive their delivery within 24 to 48 hours of completing the request form.
How much does it cost?
Standard rates with a six-month minimum: $40/mo per machine or $69/mo for a washer and dryer set. For a standard set installation, your first $69 payment is the only upfront charge. Your agreed recurring rate stays the same for the life of your uninterrupted rental.

Discounts: Save $5/month with a 12-month agreement and/or with our veteran / active-duty military discount. Either discount reduces a standard $69/month set to $64/month. Both discounts can be combined for $59/month when the customer meets both requirements.
Short term: For rentals under six months, single machines are $90/mo and sets are $95/mo. Ask us for availability and exact terms.
What discounts do you offer?
We offer two $5-per-month discounts: one for customers who sign a 12-month rental agreement, and one for eligible veterans or active-duty military customers. A standard $69/month washer and dryer set is $64/month with either discount, or $59/month when both discounts apply. These discounts are stackable; we confirm eligibility and your exact recurring rate before signup. No temporary introductory rate that expires.
How do the rental agreements work?
Standard rentals have a six-month minimum. After that, your plan continues month-to-month at the same agreed price. Separate short-term options are also available. Contact us to arrange return or pickup according to your agreement.
Is delivery and installation really free?
Yes. Standard delivery and installation in our regular Denver-area service area are free, including ordinary accessible stairs. For exceptionally difficult stairs, landings or tight spaces requiring a second person for safety, a $50 fee may apply (waived if you can provide a suitable helper). Out-of-area service may also cost extra. Any additional charge is discussed before scheduling.
What brands do you provide?
We use a rotating inventory of reliable brands like Whirlpool, Kenmore, LG and GE. While we can't guarantee a specific brand, we do guarantee a high-quality, fully tested unit that works perfectly.
What if the machine stops working?
Maintenance is 100% free for the life of your rental. If a machine has issues, we aim to repair or replace it within 3 business days at no cost to you. (Covers normal wear and tear).
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit and debit cards, including Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover. A card must be saved on file for automatic monthly payments.
What if I move?
If you move within our Denver service area, we can help relocate your machines for a small transfer fee. If you move outside our area, you can simply return the machines at the end of your current month (standard term rules apply).
