Rental Requirements

What you need to know before renting

6 Month Minimum Term

All rental agreements require a minimum commitment of 6 months. After the initial 6 months, your agreement will convert to a month-to-month arrangement.

Payment Method

All payments must be made via debit or credit card. We accept all major cards including Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover.

First Payment

Your first payment is due on the day of installation. Our technician will confirm successful installation before processing your initial payment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is delivery and install free?
Delivery and install is free for the vast majority of rentals. As long as there is direct line access to where the machines will go delivery is free. If there are stairs and landings either inside or outside of the home that cannot be navigated with the machines strapped to our dolly then there is a $50 charge to bring someone with us to assist with navigating those areas for when we drop off the machines and when we pick them up. If someone is available to help then the $50 will be waived.
What’s included in the maintenance coverage?
Our maintenance coverage includes repairs for normal wear and tear, replacement of parts, and labor costs. If your appliance cannot be repaired, we’ll replace it free of charge. We do not cover damage due to misuse or abuse.
How long are the rental agreements?
We require a 6-month minimum contract, our best pricing is available with a 12-month term. After the term period, all plans convert to month-to-month agreements with no penalties for returning. We can do shorter month-to-month agreements with adjusted pricing.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards including Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover.
How Much Does It Cost?
We charge $40 for a washer or dryer only, $65 per month for washer and dryer set. All come with free delivery, installation and maintenance for the duration of the contract. For less than six months single machines are $60/month, sets are $95/month. A $5/month discount is available for autopay enrollment. A $5/month discount is available for signing a 12-month term.
Do I Have To Pay A Down Payment?
No! The first month’s rent IS your down payment and all that is due today. Simply fill out the form and we will schedule and deliver your appliances.
Do I Get To Pick My Appliance?
No. Our appliances are on a rotating basis, therefore we cannot guarantee what brand you will get. What we will guarantee is that the set you get will work great. If they break for any reason, we will fix or replace within 3 business days.
Is There A Contract?
Yes, during delivery you will fill out a contract that states you will rent for at least 6 months and are responsible for physical damage to the equipment.
